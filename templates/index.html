How it works

  1. A small neural network (TensorFlow/Keras) estimates the price from size, rooms, locality and type.
  2. K-nearest neighbours finds the three most similar properties actually recorded in the data.
  3. K-Means places the home in one of four property groups by size and price.
  4. LDA classifies it into a lower, middle or upper price band, and PCA plots it among all properties.
